# Harbor

A calm working tool on warm grey paper. Plain type, generous rows, one teal mark for what is finished. It is not a startup landing page: no big gradients, no hero art, nothing that competes with the user's own data.

**Type.** Headings 600, sentence case, tight tracking. Body 16px at 1.55. Figures in the mono face with tabular numbers.

**Surfaces.** White cards on warm grey paper, a hairline instead of a heavy shadow, one soft shadow only for things that float.

**Controls.** Solid ink primary button, outline secondary. Teal only for done, saved, or selected.

**Voice.** Short, exact, friendly. Names the thing and the number: "12 invoices sent".

**Do not.**
- teal as a large background
- more than one accent colour
- pill-shaped primary buttons
- illustrations of people at laptops
