# Hearth

A dining room after dark: chestnut-brown paper, warm light type and one ember that glows only where a table is booked or a dish is priced. It lets a plate and a room do the talking. It is not a trendy steakhouse: no black and gold, no film-noir grain.

**Type.** Headings in the serif at 400, sentence case, large. Body 17px at 1.55 in the sans. The menu sets dish names in the heading face, descriptions in the body, and prices aligned in a right column, written plainly.

**Surfaces.** Layers of warm brown separated by hairlines. Photographs of real dishes and rooms, lit warm, cropped close, never overlaid with text on a busy area. The menu is a list, not a grid of cards.

**Controls.** Booking is an ember button with dark text and visible next free times as buttons. Secondary is outlined in ink. Inputs show the date and party size in words.

**Voice.** Warm and confident, like a host. Names the ingredient, the producer and the hour. No superlatives about the food.

**Do not.**
- a booking form buried under the menu
- ember as a page background
- fake awards and review stars
- menu items as small grey text on dark
