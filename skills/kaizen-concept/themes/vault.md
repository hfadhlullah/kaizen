# Vault

Deep green-black paper, bone-white type and one measured brass accent. It feels like a private office: slow, low-contrast surfaces and large figures. It is not a crypto dashboard: no neon, no charts that glow, no coins.

**Type.** Headings in the serif at 500, sentence case, generous size. Body 16px at 1.6 in the sans. Every amount is in mono with tabular numerals, with the currency written out and the sign shown.

**Surfaces.** Three close steps of green-black, divided by hairlines. A raised panel gets a deep soft shadow and nothing else. Charts are one line in ink and one reference line in brass, labelled directly.

**Controls.** Primary button is brass with dark text, used for one commitment per screen. Secondary is a hairline outline. Confirmations of money moving always name the amount, the account and the date.

**Voice.** Measured and exact. States fees, risks and dates without softening them. No exclamation marks, no hype about returns.

**Do not.**
- gain and loss shown only in red and green
- brass on more than one element per screen
- performance claims without a period and a source
- glow or gradients behind figures
