# Blush

Soft powder paper, a quiet flared serif and generous rounded shapes in tints of the one rosewood. It feels clean and cared-for without being clinical. It is not pink for its own sake: the tints come from product shades the user supplied, and the page has real skin, hair and texture in pictures.

**Type.** Headings in the serif at 400, sentence case, a size larger than comfort suggests. Body 17px at 1.6 in the geometric sans, light grey-brown ink. Ingredients are listed in full in the body face, never as tiny capitals.

**Surfaces.** Rounded panels in powder tints, one soft rose-tinted shadow for the raised thing. Product pictures sit on their own tint swatch, one shade per product, taken from the real pack.

**Controls.** Pill-shaped buttons: rosewood with white text for the one buying action, outlined for the rest. Shade pickers are round swatches with the shade name written beside them.

**Voice.** Gentle and specific: what it does, how it feels, how long to wait. No miracle claims, no before and after promises.

**Do not.**
- medical or anti-ageing claims
- pastel gradients across the page
- retouched or stock-skin pictures presented as results
- tiny capital ingredient lists
