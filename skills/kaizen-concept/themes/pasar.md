# Pasar

Earthy colours taken from the place itself: rice-paper background, slab headings, a chilli red and a turmeric yellow. Friendly and everyday. It is not rustic kitsch: no fake wood, no hand-drawn doodles.

**Type.** Slab headings for a printed-signboard feel, round sans body. Prices large and bold.

**Surfaces.** Warm tinted cards, real photos of the place and food, turmeric as a highlight band behind one fact.

**Controls.** Big rounded buttons, chilli red for order or book only.

**Voice.** Everyday and local, in the user's language. Prices and hours stated plainly.

**Do not.**
- hand-drawn illustrations
- stock food photos
- turmeric text on paper (fails contrast)
- English-only copy for a local market
