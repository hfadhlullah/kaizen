# Nocturne

Midnight blue ground lit from one side, wide light type, large imagery. Slow and confident. It is not glow-on-black: no neon, no orbs, light comes from the images.

**Type.** Mona Sans expanded at 300 for display, normal width at 400 for body.

**Surfaces.** Full-bleed graded images, panels as lighter steps of the ground, no borders on large areas.

**Controls.** White pill primary with dark text, ghost secondary with a hairline.

**Voice.** Few words, one big claim per screen, backed by one fact.

**Do not.**
- coloured glow
- gradient blobs
- more than one light direction
- small grey text under 14px
