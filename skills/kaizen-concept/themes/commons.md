# Commons

A cool paper with a single warm accent, bold plain headings and room for real photographs of real people. It tells a cause through its work and accounts, then asks once. It is not a guilt campaign: no crying-child imagery, no countdown timers, no hero stat of a million.

**Type.** Headings at 800, sentence case, tight. Body 18px at 1.6. Amounts are written with the currency and what they buy, in the body face.

**Surfaces.** White panels on cool paper with a hairline, no heavy shadow. Photographs are the user's own and credited, with the person's consent noted in the handoff. Reports and accounts are linked as documents, not summarised as slogans.

**Controls.** One clay button for the main gift or join action, repeated where it earns it. Donation amounts are a row of buttons plus a custom field, with one-off and monthly as a plain switch. Receipts and cancelling are one link away.

**Voice.** Plain, first person plural, honest about what is not solved. Shows where the money goes with the user's own figures.

**Do not.**
- pre-ticked monthly giving
- invented impact figures or beneficiary quotes
- a donate button on every section
- pity imagery
