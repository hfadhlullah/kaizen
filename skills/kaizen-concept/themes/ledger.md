# Ledger

A formal, dense workspace for people who are accountable for numbers. Cool white, navy, ruled tables. It is not a bank brochure: no stock handshakes, no blue gradient hero.

**Type.** Plex chosen for its clear figures and long-form legibility in forms (reason for a common face). Tabular numbers everywhere money appears.

**Surfaces.** Ruled tables, zebra rows only when rows exceed 20, square corners.

**Controls.** Navy primary, plain text secondary, destructive actions in words, never only red.

**Voice.** Formal and exact. Amounts with currency and date: "Rp 48.200.000 due 30 Nov".

**Do not.**
- playful illustrations
- rounded pills
- colour as the only status
- more than two font weights on one screen
