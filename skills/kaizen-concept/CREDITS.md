# Credits

The References step, the built-in themes, `motion.md` and `slop-check.md` were written
for kaizen after studying four open-source design skills. Their ideas were rewritten
in kaizen's own words and merged into one set of rules; where they disagreed, one rule
was chosen. No script, photo, font, theme picture or icon was taken from them. Some
passages follow their source closely, mainly in `page-anatomy.md` (Hallmark's page
anatomy, page shapes and hero postures) and `craft.md` (Impeccable's and Hallmark's
craft guides); those files are adaptations of the works below, covered by the licence
texts at the end of this file. When the user has installed one of them and picks it for a job, `outside-skills.md` says where its real tools are used; kaizen never installs them.
The theme names, token values, palettes and the preview page are kaizen's own.

## Hallmark

- Source: https://github.com/nutlope/hallmark
- Licence: MIT. Copyright (c) 2026 Hallmark contributors.
- Ideas used: themes as a material (paper, ink, type, surfaces, motion character, voice) rather than a layout; dealing three themes that differ in paper and type; the Floor / Reflex / Finish tiers and kept-with-a-reason waivers; many slop tells (eyebrows, tracked caps, one picked-out word, template hero, four common AI looks, decoration text); a fresh-context reviewer that answers with a verdict word first and few findings; "nothing invented"; the swap and squint tests.
- In: `references.md`, `themes/`, `page-anatomy.md`, `craft.md`, `copy.md`, `slop-check.md`, `motion.md`.
- Not used: its photo library (Wikimedia Commons images under their own licences), its theme pictures, its scripts (`theme.mjs`, `sloplint.mjs`).

## Taste

- Source: https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill
- Licence: MIT. Copyright (c) 2026 Leonxlnx.
- Ideas used: copy tells and the rival test; motion only with a stated purpose; turning a reference into measurable rules instead of copying its assets; capped fix rounds with written waivers.
- In: `references.md`, `copy.md`, `palettes.md`, `slop-check.md`, `motion.md`.
- Not used: its bundled photos (Unsplash and Creative Commons licences), icons, effects, scripts, and its fixed-site layout rules, which suit marketing pages but not app screens.

## Impeccable

- Source: https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable
- Licence: Apache License 2.0. Copyright 2025 Paul Bakaus.
- Ideas used: the anti-pattern list (side stripes, glow, nested cards, hero metrics, reflex fonts, the four AI looks calibration), the full set of control states, tinted neutrals, the squint test, and a critique run by an isolated reviewer.
- In: `craft.md`, `critique.md`, `copy.md`, `slop-check.md`, `motion.md`, the craft pass in `SKILL.md`.
- Changes: ideas were rewritten into kaizen's files, merged with the other sources, and narrowed to concept prototypes. No Impeccable file is included. Its `ios.md` and `android.md` (derived from ehmo's MIT `platform-design-skills`) were not used.

## Emil Kowalski

- Source: https://github.com/emilkowalski/skill
- Licence: MIT. Copyright (c) 2026 Emil Kowalski.
- Ideas used: the frequency and purpose questions before any animation; the easing curves, durations and start scales; one entrance per container and stagger caps; reduced motion that keeps opacity; the animation review checklist; worst-case data for app screens.
- In: `motion.md`, `slop-check.md`.
