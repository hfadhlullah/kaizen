# Story arc and slide recipes

The deck sells a feeling first and the product last. Never open with features.

## The five acts (12–16 slides)

| Act | Slides | Helper | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| I. World | 1–2 | `story` | Cover line about the audience's world, not the product. Scale or context (sourced stat, or none). |
| II. One scene | 1–2 | `story`, `pause` | Shrink to one person at one time ("06.45 — Gerbang sekolah"). A black `pause` slide for silence. |
| III. The pieces | 2–4 | `story`, triptych | Time-stamped scenes of the problem from each viewpoint (admin, teacher, parent…). |
| IV. The cost | 1–2 | `story` + stats | What is lost (trust, time). Stats only with a source line on the slide. |
| V. The turn | 1 | `question` | Gold slide, one italic question: "Bagaimana jika …?" |
| Reveal | 1 | `reveal` | Product name appears for the first time. |
| Features | 3–6 | `scene`, `cards` | Each scene = kicker (time/place · product), headline, 2–4 feature rows. |
| CTA | 1 | `cta` | One action (demo), contact placeholders, QR placeholder. |

Follow-up decks (shown after a story deck) skip acts I–V: open with a callback
cover ("Hari yang sama. Cerita yang berbeda."), revisit the same scenes with a
`Dulu: "<old headline>"` line (see build-features.js in the reference project), end
with a recap timeline and the CTA.

## Copy rules
- One idea per slide. Headline ≤ 2 lines at its size; if it wraps to 3, shorten the words.
- Kicker = TIME — PLACE or ACT — NAME, uppercase via helper.
- Body line is the human detail, not a feature list.
- Name the product only from the reveal onwards.
- Bahasa Indonesia by default (audience: kepala sekolah/yayasan); English if asked.
- Speaker notes: delivery cue + caveats + `Foto: <name> / Unsplash.` credit.
- Placeholders in brackets `[__]`, `[Nama]`, `[QR]` — list them in the final report.

## Layout facts (A5 landscape 8.268 × 5.827 in, margin 0.6)
- Photo backgrounds: gradient side must match where the text sits (`left` for `scene`, `bottom` for `story`).
- `scene` rows: 0.7 in pitch, 4 rows max, column width 4.3 in.
- Source lines: 8 pt at `H - 0.75`, width ≤ 6.2 in (clear of the page number).
